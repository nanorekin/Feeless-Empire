❓ How to play

Tap the pickaxe to process transactions by hand, or spend blocks to buy generators that process them automatically, every second, forever — each one you own gets a little more expensive to replicate, so variety beats stacking just one kind. Once you've built up a solid rate, open the Fork tab: resetting your blocks and generators converts your progress into permanent Genesis Shards, each worth a lasting +2% to all future production. Spend shards in Research on upgrades that stick around through every future fork too — more click power, a longer offline-earnings window, a head start after forking, and more. Blocks keep accumulating at your current rate even while the tab or browser is closed, up to your offline cap, so checking back after a break is always worth it. Nothing here has any cash value — it's a number-go-up game, not a wallet.
